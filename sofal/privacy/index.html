Sofal Privacy Policy

This page describes Sofal’s privacy practices for the App Store privacy disclosure.

Data you add to Sofal

Sofal stores pottery records you create, including piece names, clay and glaze details, notes, tags, weights, dimensions, firing details, OCR text, and photos you choose to add.

On-device storage and iCloud

Sofal stores app data on your device. If iCloud is available for the app, Sofal can sync your records through your private iCloud database so they are available across your Apple devices. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy practices apply to iCloud storage.

Camera, photos, microphone, and speech recognition

Sofal asks for camera and photo-library access so you can photograph or import pieces, glaze labels, and clay bags. Sofal asks for microphone and speech-recognition access so you can dictate studio notes. Apple’s system services may process speech recognition depending on device and OS capabilities.

Optional online AI research

Online AI research is off by default. If you enable it in Settings and add your own provider API key, Sofal may send the selected OCR text and selected label image to the provider you choose, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or OpenRouter. The provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Provider API keys are stored in the device Keychain and are not synced through Sofal’s app database.

Developer-operated services

Sofal currently does not operate developer-run accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or backend storage services.

Data deletion

You can delete records inside Sofal. You can also delete the app from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, deleting synced data may affect all devices signed in to the same iCloud account.

Contact

For support, use the Sofal support page.
