Free Go Support

Free Go is a local Go board game for iPhone with selectable 5x5, 9x9, 13x13, and 19x19 boards, local two-player play, and one-player play against an on-device AI opponent.

Common questions

  • No account is required.
  • No network connection is required for gameplay.
  • The AI opponent runs locally in the app.
  • Spoken Go phrases can be turned on or off in the app.
  • Board guide highlights can be turned on or off in the app.
