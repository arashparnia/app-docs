Free Go Privacy Policy

Free Go is a local Go board game for iPhone. It supports two-player play on the same device and one-player play against an on-device AI opponent.

Data collection

Free Go does not collect personal data. It does not use developer-operated accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or backend storage services.

Local gameplay

Game state, board selections, moves, scores, spoken phrase settings, and AI decisions are handled locally on the device. Free Go does not send gameplay data to a developer server.

AI opponent

The one-player opponent is local app logic. It does not call an online AI service and does not require an account or network connection.

Speech

Free Go can use Apple speech synthesis to speak game phrases such as atari, capture, ko, and territory scoring. Speech output is generated by Apple system functionality on the device.

Tracking and advertising

Free Go does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Contact

For support, use the Free Go support page.
