# Name shortlist

Date: 2026-11-03. Forty candidates were brainstormed, fifteen were checked, ten were shortlisted, and an adversarial second agent re-checked the ten (`design/naming.md` has all forty and the raw check results). Brief: fast, effortless, trustworthy ("safe to throw the paper away"), calm, modern; a consumer App Store product first and a service later; not confusable with Pack Rat, Sofal, Free Go, or any of the ~60 products in the competitive survey (in particular the Scan, Paperless, Doc, Snap, Lens, Scannable, Dext and Doxie families).

## How the checks were run, and what they are not

- Domains: RDAP lookups (`rdap.org` for .com/.app/.ai; the Identity Digital registry server for .io). 200 = registered, 404 = no record, which usually means available but can also mean reserved or premium. Several lookups hit rate limits and are marked unknown.
- App Store: Apple's iTunes Search API for the exact name and its two-word variant (US store, software only).
- Trademarks: Trademarkia's mirror of the USPTO database, exact word, first page only, no phonetic search. The USPTO, TMview and Justia refused automated access.
- Not run: general web search, Google Play, Product Hunt, GitHub search (the shared web-search budget was exhausted). "Clear" below means "nothing found in the App Store catalog, the US trademark database or on the domain", not a clearance opinion. Before committing, spend an hour on Google Play, Product Hunt and a trademark attorney's knockout search.

## Rejected along the way (so you do not re-propose them)

Quire (Potix owns a class 42 mark and "Quire: Document & QR Scanner" exists), Sheaf (four or more "Sheaf" PDF scanner apps), Ream (two Ream scanner apps and a pending class 9 mark), Vellum ("Vellum Scanner" plus Vellum Wellness marks), Quirely, Paperlift ("Paperlift: Scan Paper to Data"), Pagefall (developer "Pagefall limited"), Sheafly and Foliary (exact-name apps), and the single words Sift, Skim, Shed, Settle, Whisk, Hush, Folio and Kami (90 to 720 USPTO hits each and ten or more identically named apps).

## My recommendation

- If you want a clean start with no caveats: **Papersieve**. Register papersieve.com/.app/.io/.ai now (they are cheap while free) and use the GitHub slug `papersieve`.
- If brand quality matters more than owning the .com: **Papershed**. Register papershed.app/.io/.ai, use `papershed` as the slug, and make an offer on the .com later if the product works. The App Store and trademark checks were clean.
- If neither resonates, **Quietpaper** and **Afterpaper** are clean and both .coms are for sale rather than in use.
